About Mandy's Saloon

Your Beauty, Our Passion

Mandy's Saloon is a premium yet affordable hair and beauty salon located at 10 Park Ln, Parktown, Johannesburg. Since 2018, we have been making women in Parktown, Rosebank, Braamfontein and Johannesburg CBD feel confident and beautiful.


We specialize in protective hairstyles including knotless braids, fulani braids, straight back and straight up, as well as professional nail sets and cluster lashes. Our salon is known for neat work, affordable prices and friendly service.
